# Does Price Remember Where It Turned?

A research note by Zihan (Eric) Lin, October 2026.

**Read it:** https://faustzi.github.io/price-memory/ · [PDF](does-price-remember-where-it-turned.pdf)

## Summary

Do large one-way moves in E-mini S&P 500 futures start more often from recent turns that price has not broken, and from prices where little has traded, than from other prices at the same time? The note states a trader-behaviour explanation, derives seven testable predictions, and measures each against fake markets: the real one-minute path cut into 13-minute blocks whose directions are flipped at random, which keep volatility and its clustering but erase any memory of price.

A two-term model fitted on 2022–2023 was frozen before the held-out year was read. In that year, within each trading day, the model's top tenth of places went on to move eight times the session's typical swing 19.8% of the time, against 11.1% for the rest of the day, a larger gap than in any of 20 fake markets. The note also reports the explanations that did not hold, nine ways the measurement fooled itself, and what the data cannot show.
